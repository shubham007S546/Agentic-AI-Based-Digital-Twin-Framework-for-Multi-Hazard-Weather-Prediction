Platform Capabilities

Not a weather dashboard. A complete disaster intelligence system.

Rainfall & Cloudburst Prediction

Multi-horizon precipitation forecasting from classical ML to Temporal Fusion Transformers, tuned on Himalayan monsoon dynamics.

Map-First GIS Workspace

A live geospatial canvas with satellite, terrain, hydrology, infrastructure and AI risk layers — the map is the platform.

Digital Twin

A living virtual replica of terrain, rivers and infrastructure enabling historical replay and future scenario simulation.

Explainable AI

SHAP values, attention heatmaps and variable importance make every prediction transparent to scientists and officials.

Agentic AI Assistant

Autonomous agents that monitor data streams, reason about risk, answer questions and draft situational reports.

Early Warning System

Village-level alerting with confidence intervals and lead times up to 72 hours for disaster management authorities.

Research Roadmap

From raw datasets to an early warning platform

Each stage of the pipeline builds toward a fully agentic Digital Twin decision-support system for Himachal Pradesh and beyond.

  1. Dataset Collection

    Complete

    17 collectors across weather, satellite, hydrology, terrain and disaster records

  2. Data Validation

    Complete

    Complete
